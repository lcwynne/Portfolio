Project Overview

Proposal

Machine Learning was originally designed and built for a technical audience. Business Analysts struggled to use the product and make models for their use cases and we needed to simplify this product and include it' advanced features.

Goals

Redesign Machine Learning with a non-technical business analyst in mind. Simplify the product by reducing the number of steps it takes a user to build and use a model. Guide users with suggestions and provide an optional path for users to utilize advanced features to fine-tune their models.

Solution

We took a seven-step product to a 3-step product and incorporated design pattern that are more familiar to our current customers for easier adoption. Advanced features were placed into a panel on the right side of the product and enabled advanced users to continue to fine-tune models as needed.

Design Process - Highlights

Design Workshop

As a UX-driven project, we had to influence stakeholders such as our product managers and engineers that a redesign would help product adoption and ease use for our target audience. We did this by pulling in telemetry data that showed an early drop off rate and convinced these stakeholders that we needed an in-person workshop to alleviate user pain points throughout the user flow.

‍

After identifying themes from the 7-step flow, we were able to guide the team to discover a 3-step flow to reduce time to wow.

Confirm New Direction

At the time of this proposal, we had a unique opportunity to conduct in-person user testing at the company's conference in Amsterdam. We tested the newly identified 3-step approach for users by having participants list what functionality they expected to do in each step.

Overall, participants responded positively to the new 3-step approach and were able to identify the necessary steps to build a model under each category. Advanced participants liked the idea of utilizing technology to streamline the model building process and being able to manipulate the model in settings rather than a longer user flow.

High Fidelity Proposal

After user confirmation, I designed the high fidelity proposal for the team.

Lessons Learned

  • Mentor and delegate work to amore junior designer
  • Facilitate workshop with high-level leadership involved
  • Advanced Tech like ML is hard to understand for english speakers; when testing in Amsterdam with non-native English speakers, copy became a top concern in the redesign
  • One of the hardest design challenges is to help users formulate a question or prompt

Next Steps

After delivery of this redesign, the project was handed over to another designer for continued work past MVP. The product managers and engineers are continuing to work towards this vision in smaller phases.
