Project Overview

The Proposal

Senior leadership challenged us to explore how we could use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies, like ChatGPT, in our company and it's multiple products. Our goal was to explore and design a way for Alteryx to make their own version of ChatGPT for it's multi-product suite.

Goals

The organization needed a vision that included advanced artificial intelligence (AI) technology integrated into current products to ease workflow creation amongst multiple collaborators. The feature created should include a chat-like interface and be able to work for multiple personas including a business analysts, a data scientist, and a manager. In addition the feature would need to support the current Alteryx canvas, code, and a way for users to use real-time collaboration. This prototype would beed to be delivered within a week to C-level executives.

Solution + Prototype

While working with UX leadership and a co-designer, I lead the strategic design vision for the design by crafting high-fidelity designs that highlighted a chat-like interface available to all personas and showed collaborators. The design included three views - whiteboard, code, and canvas - that could display the workflow depending on the persona's preference. In addition to leading the vision, I also presented the prototype to our Chief Product Officer and his direct reports throughout the week and iterated on feedback quickly.

Design Process - Highlights

Identifying Personas

We wanted to provide a way for multiple personas to take advantage of an Artificial Intelligence feature within our product suite. We had to include a way for a leader, a business analyst, and a data scientist to benefit from this new technology.

‍

Creating a User Flow

We created a user flow that would have leadership ask the chat feature a question to begin an analysis, then an analyst is able to take that question and add supporting analyses on the response from the AI-chat tool. A data scientist would validate that the analyses were true and accurate. The leader would end up with a report of the analyses with details about the process her team took to validate each step. Lessons Learned

  • Working with C-suite level stakeholders and iterating fast
  • Delegate work to other team members so that I could focus on the overall user flow instead of component updates or prototyping
  • Presenting and delivering a prototype to C-Suite executives multiple times
  • Navigating AI challenges, like data privacy and helping users formulate a prompt to the co-pilot

Next Steps

This work was reciprocated well by stakeholders and is used at the guiding vision for a company project, AiDIN Co-Pilot. I was able to hand this project off to another designer before starting to work on the Multi-Product Experience initiative for Alteryx.
