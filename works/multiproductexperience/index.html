Project Overview

Proposal

The organization has built a suite of products and users need to be able to use their data across these products seamlessly. Users need help navigating from product to product and knowing how moving from one product affects their assets.

Goals

Identify a solution for users to move from one product to another and understand where they are in the platform. Ensure users can navigate upstream and downstream in their user journey and communicate how assets are being used. Communicate with all products to ensure user needs are met and designs are compatible across the suite.

Challenge: How can we create a point-to-point business model when we have hub and spoke? How can users move seamlessly when application accept different types of data files?

Solution

The solution to this proposal includes multiple features and a possible ecosystem redesign that are still being explored, but one feature that adds value to the user right now is called contextual navigation. The ribbon (highlighted in pink) is a way for users to move around the platform with their assets.

Design Process - Highlights

Research

Our team conducted a study focusing on improving the experience of navigating between different cloud applications. The ribbon was part of the study and was received very well with users for it's "universal workflow' feel and findability.

"It doesn't take that much space, you don't have to do an extra click. Actually, it falls in line with all the other ways that Alteryx works. Alteryx gives you a trail of breadcrumbs no matter what you're doing." - Participant who found the ribbon design extremely useful
User Flows

I took a user case and created a user flow through the Alteryx product suite. I had to account for todays technical limitations as well as what the technical roadblocks that could be resolved to make this transition for the user seamless. Some of these challenges included applications not being able to accept certain data files or not being able to recognize components from an application within the product suite.

Ideation

To keep a user informed about their user journey through the platform, we ideated on ways to showcase the users flow.

The issue we had with our current solution was that this solved for a linear user journey. Most users will havea non-linear journey through the platform and we had to strategically account for these use cases by exploring non-linear solutions. Our original idea was to present each user journey as linear, but allow a user to expand to explore in the case that the journey was non-linear. After designing a few mock ups using our use-cases from our research study, we decided that a user will need a different approach.

Lessons Learned (so far)

  • Applying strategic thinking skills by working with multiple product managers to align roadmaps on design
  • Breaking down designs to MVP from 5+ year vision

Next Steps

Continue to iterate on designs with our visual design team and work with architects and Product Managers of other products to utilize current technology and provide this feature to users quickly to allow for feedback and iteration.
