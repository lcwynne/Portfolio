Why static mockups failed

Draft · not confirmed

Draft: Agentic flows only make sense when you can click through them, because previewing AI changes, approving, and undoing are the design. The sharper problem was findability: new AI design tools were shipping constantly (Claude Code, Claude Design, Cursor, Figma's AI), so designers prototyped in different tools, and with several designers per product and a fast-shipping org, leadership could not tell where the latest prototype was.
