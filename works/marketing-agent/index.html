Draft · not confirmed

Draft, the hardest call: Customers told me they would not send a generated email as-is; they always wanted to edit it in Klaviyo's editor. Because of how the backend worked, we could not open the real editor directly, and we wanted to ship fast. We built an intermediate review step where users could change everything that was not content, such as the audience, and sent content edits through to the editor. What we gave up: a seamless edit. The direct path was never built because the product was sunset first.
