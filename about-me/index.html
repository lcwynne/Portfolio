My Design Story

I started my design career after a long journey of researching human-computer interactions (HCI), building front-end websites, developing Python coding classes for intelligence analysts, and creating Lean-Startup workshops in government, academia, and the private sector.

Working my way from designing websites and applications in Microsoft Paint to being the lead designer piecing together a multi-product suite, I learned that I enjoy the challenge of chasing down answers to difficult questions and having hard conversations about strategic goals. I believe that leaning into resistance provides an opportunity for learning and helps create transparency between stakeholders.
